## Access

Use Data Explorer for a bounded point time series, or the data directory/web services for a chosen variable, resolution and date. Retain the ZIP metadata and stability designation. Select one resolution explicitly.

[Provider/access documentation](https://prism.oregonstate.edu/) · [Citation/product reference](https://prism.oregonstate.edu/)

## Reproducibility and use

Record the exact release, variables, units, dates, geographic selection, retrieval time and source citation. Retain original quality flags and metadata. Follow [data governance](../docs/governance.md); source terms apply independently of this repository's MIT license.

Verified details: [resolutions](https://prism.oregonstate.edu/orders/) and [source formats](https://prism.oregonstate.edu/formats/).
